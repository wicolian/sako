# Go-to-market: the first 30 days

A founder-led launch plan for a business with no paid media, no audience and one goal: land the first paying client.

## How to use this

- Use it when you are about to launch a B2B service and nobody knows you yet.
- Copy the phase table. Replace our posts with your own. Keep the structure.
- Treat the checkpoints as tripwires. If you miss one, do the action next to it. Do not wait and hope.

## Read this first: what really happened

We wrote this plan with a 30-day target for the first paying client. In real life, our first revenue came about nine months after we started (we began at the end of 2024 and had no revenue until September 2025).

So treat "30 days" as a stretch target. The plan is still a good way to make noise in a short time. But do not build your cash plan on it. Plan your runway for a much longer wait.

## The one KPI

One paying client on a plan of about $2,500 a month by day 30.

Views, likes and followers do not count unless they lead to a conversation with a buyer.

## The timeline

| Phase | Days | Goal | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Pre-heat | -5 to 0 | Build curiosity. Plant the brand flag. | 5 posts |
| 1. Launch week | 1 to 7 | Go loud. Show your face. Create proof. | 6 posts |
| 2. Proof and conversion | 8 to 21 | Show it works. Show real delivery and metrics. | 9 posts |
| 3. Close and expand | 22 to 30 | Close one client. Start the next pipeline. | 5 posts |

About 25 posts in total. Roughly three posts a week on each founder profile and two a week on the company page.

## Week by week

### Week 0 (days -5 to 0): pre-heat

Goal: make people curious before launch.

- [ ] Founder teaser: "We have been building quietly..."
- [ ] Company page post: one line on what the company is, plus the tagline.
- [ ] Behind-the-scenes photo of the chaos you are fixing.
- [ ] Poll: let people vote on two taglines.
- [ ] Short teaser video (about 8 seconds): logo and tagline. "Full drop on Monday."

Rules:

- Post every other day. Alternate between founders.
- Reply to every comment by hand within 30 minutes.
- Message anyone who reacts with curiosity. Move them to email.

### Week 1 (days 1 to 7): the drop

Goal: announce loudly. Make the company feel real.

- [ ] Hero film (see the brand playbook).
- [ ] Founder story: why we built this.
- [ ] Explainer: how a request becomes a delivery (carousel or short video).
- [ ] Proof dashboard preview: show the SLA and delivery metrics in public.
- [ ] Launch recap and thank-you post. Say "founding client spots are open".
- [ ] Live Q&A thread.

KPIs for the week:

- 1,000 or more views on the hero film
- 20 or more comments
- 5 or more serious DMs
- 3 discovery calls booked by day 7

Tripwire: no qualified lead by day 7? Message 10 warm contacts directly. Send the hero video and a short pitch.

### Week 2 (days 8 to 14): proof in motion

Goal: show it working in a real context.

- [ ] "First project in progress" (blur anything private).
- [ ] "Inside our dashboard": a short metrics clip.
- [ ] "How we deliver in 48 hours": a step-by-step breakdown.
- [ ] "Behind the brand: how we work without meetings."
- [ ] "One week since launch: what is real." Be honest. Say what is not working yet.

KPIs for the week:

- 2 live client calls
- 1 verbal yes or a pilot in progress

Tripwire: nothing? Put a small paid boost behind the hero film. Target design ops and SaaS marketing roles.

### Week 3 (days 15 to 21): proof over hype

Goal: show traction and repeatability. The tone moves from excitement to trust.

- [ ] Client result: a before and after carousel.
- [ ] A short client quote or beta tester quote.
- [ ] A clip of your SLA monitoring tool at work.
- [ ] A long post: "Why reliability beats creativity."

KPIs for the week:

- 1 paying client
- 1 inbound lead for the next client
- 1,000 or more followers across founders and company page

### Week 4 (days 22 to 30): close and compound

Goal: close the first client in public. Share what you learned. Reopen the pipeline.

- [ ] "Our first client is live."
- [ ] "What we learned in 30 days." No fluff. Use data.
- [ ] Client work showcase.
- [ ] The manifesto film, now that you have proof.
- [ ] "We are open for 3 new clients": a clear call to action with a limited founder price.

KPIs for the period:

- 1 confirmed client (signed or paid)
- 2 leads in follow-up
- 1 partnership inquiry

## Where each thing lives

| Channel | How often | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Founder profiles | 3 to 4 posts a week | Personality, trust, DM conversion |
| Company page | 2 posts a week | Brand consistency, official updates |
| Website or landing page | Always on | The place where visitors convert. Host the hero film and the proof dashboard. |
| Video hosting site | As needed | Long videos for press and embeds |
| Blog or newsletter (optional) | One long manifesto | Search traffic and thought leadership |

Mix of content: about 60% from founders, 30% from the company page, 10% proof and client work.

Best posting times (India time): 9:00 to 10:30 AM for B2B engagement. 5:00 to 7:00 PM for founder stories. Adapt these to the time zone of your buyers.

## The conversion flow

Post, then comment, then DM, then a 15-minute discovery call, then a PDF quote from your simple master agreement.

### DM script (template)

> Hey [name], I saw you run [their function]. We just launched [company]: [one-line offer]. Want a founding-client pilot? [Delivery promise], no meetings.

### Call to action for every post

- "DM me if you want [your outcome]."
- "We are opening [number] founding client slots this month."

## Survival checkpoints

| Day | What you need | If you miss it |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 1 warm lead | DM 10 people you already know. By hand. |
| 14 | 1 discovery call booked | Boost the hero film with a small budget. |
| 21 | 1 pilot or signed agreement | Post a public ask: "We are opening 1 slot for a founding client." |
| 30 | 1 paying client | Extend the campaign by 15 days. Push a case study. |

## Hard truths from the plan

- Comment three times more than you post. This is how you get seen early.
- Plan for the first client to come from a DM or a call, not from a post.
- Do not chase followers. Chase replies, intros and credibility.
- Calm confidence builds trust faster than any ad.

## Adapt it

- Replace "48 hours" and "no meetings" with your own promise.
- If your buyers are not on LinkedIn, move the plan to the place they do read.
- If you are a solo founder, cut the founder split. Post under one profile and double the DMs.
- Write the full content calendar before day -5: date, title, outline, visual, call to action, metric. Do not improvise daily.
- Pair this with the sales playbook. Posts create attention. Calls close clients.
