# The launch video (late 2024)

An 80-second video Koushik recorded before the beta launch. It is the clearest statement of what Sako
set out to be. Transcribed and lightly cleaned.

> Six months ago, I quit my full-time job. It was comfortable, maybe too comfortable.
>
> Then I started freelancing full-time, and for a while it felt great: freedom, creativity. But something
> was missing. Every project felt like déjà vu. Same chaos, new client.
>
> So I teamed up with my co-founder and we asked one simple question: why are creative teams still
> working like 2010 agencies?
>
> And then we flipped the model. What if design worked like a product? Fast, async and predictable.
>
> So we built Sako, a design subscription that actually scales. Transparent pricing, predictable
> turnaround, no calls, no chasing. Just quality design, delivered in under 48 hours, every time.
>
> We are opening beta access to a few early brands who want to try product-subscription design before
> we launch in January. If you believe design should be predictable, we built this for you.

## What happened next

| When | What |
|---|---|
| End of 2024 | Koushik and Sanjay start Sako. Beta for a few early brands. |
| January 2025 | Public launch. |
| January to August 2025 | Building, selling, iterating. **No revenue.** |
| September 2025 | First revenue. It starts to pick up. |
| September 2025 to April 2026 | About **$7,000 a month**, **10–40%** revenue growth. A team of 4 designers, 1 HR person and 1 growth hacker. |
| Around April 2026 | We sell the clients (not the code) and close. We did not love the agency work, and distribution was the hard part. |
| October 2026 | Everything is made public (source-available): the software, the automations and the playbooks. |

The promise in the video ("no calls, no chasing, under 48 hours") is what the software in this repo
does: the client portal, the studio queue, the SLA clock and the day credits.
