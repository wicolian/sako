<LegalContent title="Refund Policy">

**Last Updated:** December 28, 2025
**Effective Date:** December 28, 2025

This Refund Policy explains when and how you can request a refund for Sako ("Service"), operated by [Creative Ops, Inc.](https://www.creativeops.in/) ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a **14-day money-back guarantee** for new subscribers.

### 1.1 Eligibility
You're eligible if:
- This is your **first subscription** to Sako
- You request the refund **within 14 days** of your first payment
- Your account is in good standing (no AUP violations)

### 1.2 What's Covered
The guarantee applies to:
- Your **first month's subscription fees** only
- Core, Ops Base, or Enterprise plans

### 1.3 What's Not Covered
The guarantee does **not** apply to:
- Second or subsequent subscriptions
- Refund requests after 14 days
- Accounts terminated for policy violations
- Custom enterprise contracts with different terms
- Add-on services purchased separately

## 2. How to Request a Refund

### 2.1 Contact Us
Send your refund request to:

**Email:** billing@trysako.com  
**Subject:** Refund Request

Include:
- Your account email
- Subscription start date
- Reason for the refund (optional but helpful)

### 2.2 Processing Time
We process refund requests within **5 business days**. Once approved:
- Credit card refunds appear within 5-10 business days
- ACH refunds appear within 3-5 business days

### 2.3 What Happens to Your Account
After a refund:
- Your subscription is immediately canceled
- Access to the Service ends
- Any deliverables in progress will be delivered if substantially complete
- Your account data is retained for 30 days before deletion

## 3. Cancellation vs. Refund

### 3.1 Cancellation
You can cancel your subscription anytime with **7 days notice** before your next billing cycle:
- Access continues until the end of your paid period
- No partial refunds for unused time
- Deliverables in progress are completed

### 3.2 Refund
Refunds are only available during the 14-day money-back guarantee period for first-time subscribers.

## 4. SLA Credits

Separate from refunds, you may receive **SLA credits** if we miss our delivery commitments:

### 4.1 How Credits Work
- Credits are applied to your next billing cycle
- Credits don't accumulate beyond one month's fees
- Credits cannot be converted to cash refunds

### 4.2 Credit vs. Refund
SLA credits are **not refunds**. They're compensation for specific missed deadlines and don't affect your eligibility for the money-back guarantee.

See our full [Service Level Agreement](/legal/sla) for credit terms.

## 5. Pause vs. Refund

### 5.1 Pausing Your Subscription
Instead of canceling, you can **pause** your subscription:
- Unused days are banked (up to 30 days per cycle)
- Banked days expire after 90 days
- Resume anytime without restarting your subscription

### 5.2 When to Pause
Consider pausing if:
- You're temporarily busy and can't submit requests
- You want to take a break but plan to return
- You're between projects or campaigns

Pausing preserves your account, team setup, and brand assets.

## 6. Disputes & Chargebacks

### 6.1 Contact Us First
If you have billing concerns, please contact us at billing@trysako.com before filing a dispute with your bank or card issuer.

We can often resolve issues faster than the chargeback process.

### 6.2 Chargeback Consequences
Chargebacks filed without first contacting us may result in:
- Immediate account suspension
- Loss of access to deliverables
- Ineligibility for future refunds or credits
- Collection of any amounts owed

## 7. Enterprise Customers

Enterprise customers with custom contracts may have different refund terms. Your Master Service Agreement (MSA) or Order Form takes precedence over this policy.

Contact your account manager or enterprise@trysako.com for refund inquiries.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy by:
- Posting the new version at this URL
- Notifying you via email or in the Service

Changes apply to subscriptions started after the update date. Existing subscribers retain the terms in effect when they subscribed.
