## 4. AI, Sako Composer & Your Data

### 4.1 How Composer Works
Sako Composer uses AI models to generate design drafts based on your inputs (briefs, brand assets, references).

**What we send to AI systems:**
- Text from your briefs
- Brand assets you upload
- Style preferences and constraints
- Previous feedback on similar requests

**What we don't do:**
- **We don't train public AI models** with your content
- **We don't share your content** with other customers
- **We don't sell your data** to third-party AI companies

### 4.2 AI Service Providers
We use third-party AI services (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Replicate, etc.) to power Composer. These providers process your inputs **only to generate your drafts** and don't retain your data for their own training (per our agreements with them).

### 4.3 Human Review
Our designers review all AI-generated drafts before delivery. They may see your briefs and brand materials to ensure quality.

### 4.4 Improving Composer
We may analyze **aggregated, anonymized usage patterns** (e.g., "brief length correlates with approval rates") to improve Composer. We don't use your specific content or designs.

### 5.2 Legal Requirements
We may disclose data if required by:

- Law enforcement investigations

### 5.3 Business Transfers
If we merge with, are acquired by, or sell assets to another company, your data may transfer to the new entity. You'll be notified and can delete your account before the transfer.

### 5.4 With Your Consent
We may share data in other ways if you explicitly authorize it (e.g., publishing your case study, sharing deliverables with your team members).

### 5.5 Aggregated Data
We may share **anonymized, aggregated statistics** (e.g., "80% of users request social media assets") publicly or with partners. This data can't identify you.

**We never sell your personal data to third parties.**

## 6. Data Security

We take security seriously and implement multiple layers of protection:

### 6.1 Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption in transit:** TLS 1.3 for all connections
- **Encryption at rest:** AES-256 for stored data
- **Secure infrastructure:** We use reputable cloud providers (Railway, Neon, Cloudflare R2, Clerk). Our cloud providers maintain their own certifications. We have not completed SOC 2 certification ourselves.
- **Access controls:** Role-based permissions, 2FA for team accounts
- **Audit logging:** Track who accessed what and when

### 6.2 Operational Safeguards
- Background checks for employees with data access
- Regular security training
- Incident response plan
- Internal monitoring and basic security scanning
- Industry-standard security practices

### 6.3 Limitations
**No system is 100% secure.** While we use industry-standard measures, we can't guarantee absolute security. You're responsible for:
- Keeping your password confidential
- Using strong, unique passwords
- Enabling two-factor authentication (if available)
- Logging out on shared devices

**Report security issues immediately** to security@trysako.com.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as necessary:

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Account info** | Active account + 90 days after cancellation |
| **Project files & deliverables** | Active account + 1 year (then archived) |
| **Payment records** | 7 years (tax/legal requirements) |
| **Support tickets** | 3 years |
| **Usage logs** | 2 years |
| **Marketing data** | Until you unsubscribe + 30 days |

**After deletion:**
- Data is removed from active systems within 30 days
- Backups are purged within 90 days
- Some data may persist in archives for legal/accounting purposes

**You can request early deletion** by emailing privacy@trysako.com.
