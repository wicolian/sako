## 1. What Are Cookies?

**Cookies** are small text files that websites place on your device (computer, phone, tablet) to store information about your visit. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the site.

### Types of Tracking Technologies

Beyond traditional cookies, we may also use:

- **Local Storage:** Data stored in your browser (similar to cookies but larger capacity)
- **Session Storage:** Temporary data that's cleared when you close your browser
- **Pixels/Web Beacons:** Tiny invisible images that track page views or email opens
- **JavaScript:** Code that runs in your browser to collect usage data

**For simplicity, we refer to all of these as "cookies" in this policy.**

## 2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

- **Keep you logged in** to your account (session management)
- **Remember your preferences** (theme, language, settings)
- **Understand how you use Sako** (analytics, feature usage)
- **Improve performance** (load balancing, caching)
- **Prevent fraud and abuse** (security, spam detection)
- **Provide customer support** (error tracking, debugging)

We **do not** use cookies to:
- Track you across unrelated websites
- Sell your data to third parties
- Build advertising profiles for other companies

**Purpose:** Help us understand how you use Sako so we can improve it.

**Legal Basis:** Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a))

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Consent Needed? |

**What PostHog tracks:**

**Privacy note:** PostHog is **privacy-focused** and can be self-hosted.

**What Google Analytics tracks:**
- Page views and navigation patterns
- Time spent on pages

- Conversion events (sign-ups, form submissions)

**What Google Analytics does NOT track:**
- Personally identifiable information
- Your specific interactions with content
- Individual user behavior patterns (data is aggregated)

**Privacy note:** We use Google Analytics 4 with consent mode v2, which respects your privacy choices and only collects data when you grant consent. This includes ad personalization controls and user data consent settings as required by GDPR and CCPA.

### 3.3 Error Monitoring Cookies

**Purpose:** Detect and fix bugs, crashes, and errors.

**Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Consent Needed? |
|-------------|----------|---------|----------|-----------------|
| `sentry-trace` | Sentry | Track error context across requests | Session | Optional* |
| `sentry-session` | Sentry | Group errors by user session | Session | Optional* |

**What Sentry tracks:**
- JavaScript errors and crashes

**Privacy note:** We anonymize personally identifiable information (PII) in error reports. If an error message contains your email or name, it's automatically redacted.

**\*Optional but recommended:** Technically legitimate interest, but you can opt out below if preferred.

## 5. How to Control Cookies

You have several options to manage cookies:

### 5.1 Cookie Preferences (Recommended)

**[Adjust Cookie Settings](#)** *(link to your cookie banner settings)*

Use our cookie banner to:
- **Accept all** cookies
- **Reject optional** cookies (analytics, marketing)
- **Customize** which categories you allow

**Note:** Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled.

### 5.2 Browser Settings

All major browsers let you block or delete cookies:

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

**Learn how:**
- [Chrome Cookie Settings](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox Cookie Settings](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari Cookie Settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge Cookie Settings](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

**Blocking all cookies may break functionality** (you won't be able to log in or make payments).

### 5.3 Opt-Out Links

**PostHog (Analytics):**
- PostHog respects **Do Not Track (DNT)** browser signals
- Or opt out directly: [PostHog Opt-Out](https://posthog.com/docs/privacy#opt-out)

**Sentry (Error Monitoring):**
- Contact [support@trysako.com](mailto:support@trysako.com) to opt out
- Note: May reduce our ability to fix bugs affecting your account

### 5.4 Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers have a **"Do Not Track"** setting. We honor DNT signals for **PostHog analytics** (it will not track you if DNT is enabled).

**How to enable DNT:**
- **Chrome:** Not supported (use extensions like Privacy Badger)
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Send "Do Not Track" signal
- **Safari:** Enabled by default on iOS/macOS

## 9. Cookie Lifespan

### Session Cookies
Deleted when you **close your browser**. Used for temporary data like login sessions.

### Persistent Cookies
Remain on your device for a **set period** or until you delete them manually.

| Duration | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Session** | Authentication, load balancing |
| **30 minutes** | Stripe checkout |
| **1 year** | PostHog analytics, Stripe fraud detection |
| **Until you delete** | User preferences (if we add them) |

**You can delete all cookies anytime** via your browser settings (Section 5.2).
