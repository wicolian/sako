**Last Updated:** December 24, 2025

[Creative Ops, Inc.](https://www.creativeops.in/) ("Company," "Sako," "we," "us," or "our") uses carefully selected third-party sub-processors to deliver our services. This page lists all sub-processors that may process customer data, including personal data.

All sub-processors are bound by **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)** and maintain appropriate security certifications. For EU/UK customers, we implement **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** for international transfers.

| Sub-Processor | Purpose | Data Processed | Location | Certifications |

| **Railway Inc.** | Website and application hosting, CDN, edge functions | Account data, usage data, project files | United States | Built on AWS infrastructure, GDPR compliant |

## 10. Sub-Processor Obligations

All sub-processors must:

### 10.1 Contractual Requirements
- Sign **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)** or equivalent
- Implement **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** for international transfers (EU/UK customers)
- Process data **only on our instructions**
- **Not use customer data** for their own purposes (except as required to provide services)

### 10.2 Security Requirements
- Maintain **appropriate technical and organizational measures** (TOMs)
- Hold relevant **security certifications** where available (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Implement **encryption** in transit and at rest
- Follow **industry-standard security practices**
- Provide **breach notification** within 24 hours of discovery

**Note:** Not all sub-processors have formal certifications like SOC 2. We evaluate their security practices and infrastructure before engagement.

### 10.3 Data Subject Rights
- Assist with **data subject access requests** (DSARs)
- Support **deletion, correction, and portability** requests
- Cooperate with **supervisory authority inquiries**

### 10.4 Termination
- **Delete or return** all customer data upon contract termination
- Provide **certification of deletion** upon request

## 11. Our Due Diligence Process

Before engaging any sub-processor, we:

### 11.1 Security Assessment
- Review **security certifications** (SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, etc.)
- Assess **data handling practices** and infrastructure
- Verify **encryption standards** (TLS 1.3, AES-256, etc.)
- Evaluate **incident response procedures**

### 11.2 Legal Review
- Review **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)**
- Ensure **GDPR/CCPA compliance**
- Verify **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** for international transfers
- Check **insurance coverage** (cyber liability, E&O)

### 11.3 Ongoing Monitoring
- Annual **re-certification review**
- Quarterly **security posture checks**
- Monitor for **data breaches or incidents**
- Track **compliance with SLAs**

## 12. Changes to Sub-Processors

### 12.1 Adding or Replacing Sub-Processors
We may add or replace sub-processors as we improve our services. Changes are made only after:
- Completing due diligence (Section 11)
- Signing Data Processing Agreements
- Implementing appropriate safeguards

### 12.2 Notification Timeline

**All Customers:**
- **30 days advance notice** for new sub-processors that process personal data
- Notice via email to your account email
- Update to this page (check "Last Updated" date)

**Enterprise Customers with DPAs:**
- **Email notification** 30 days before engagement
- Right to **object** for legitimate data protection reasons
- Alternative solutions or **contract termination** if objection cannot be accommodated

### 12.3 How to Object
If you're an Enterprise customer and object to a new sub-processor:

**Email:** [legal@trysako.com](mailto:legal@trysako.com)  
**Subject:** Sub-Processor Objection  
**Include:**
- Sub-processor name
- Specific data protection concerns
- Proposed alternatives (if any)

**Response:** Within 10 business days, we'll either:
- Provide additional safeguards to address concerns
- Not engage the sub-processor
- Allow contract termination without penalty (prorated refund)

### 12.4 Subscribe to Notifications
Enterprise customers can subscribe to **automated sub-processor change notifications**:

**Email:** [legal@trysako.com](mailto:legal@trysako.com)  
**Subject:** Subscribe to Sub-Processor Notifications

## 16. Sub-Processor Incidents & Breaches

### 16.1 Incident Notification
If a sub-processor experiences a data breach affecting your data:
- **Sub-processor notifies us:** Within 24 hours (per our DPAs)
- **We notify you:** Within 24 hours of learning of the breach
- **We notify authorities:** As required by GDPR/applicable law (within 72 hours)

### 16.2 What We Provide
- **Nature of the breach** (what data, how many affected)
- **Likely consequences** and risk assessment
- **Measures taken** by sub-processor and us to mitigate
- **Recommended actions** for you (if any)

### 16.3 Historical Incidents
**No breaches to date.** This page will be updated if any sub-processor incidents affect customer data.
