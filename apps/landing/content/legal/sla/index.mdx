**Last Updated:** December 24, 2025
**Effective Date:** December 24, 2025

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") describes [Creative Ops, Inc.](https://www.creativeops.in/)'s ("Company," "Sako," "we," "us," or "our") commitment to turnaround times, quality standards, and remedies available if we fail to meet our commitments.

This SLA is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and applies to all paid subscriptions.

## 1. Our Core Commitment

**We guarantee a 48-hour turnaround on all Active tasks.**

This means:
- **First draft delivered** within 48 business hours
- **Visible in your dashboard** with a real-time countdown
- **Automatic credits** if we miss the deadline
- **No exceptions or fine print** for standard tasks

***

## 2. The 48-Hour SLA Explained

### 2.1 What Qualifies as "48 Business Hours"

**Business hours:**
- **Monday - Friday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM Eastern Time (EST/EDT)**
- Excludes weekends (Saturday, Sunday)
- Excludes U.S. federal holidays (see Section 2.2)

**Calculation example:**
- Task goes Active: **Monday 3:00 PM EST**
- 48 business hours = 6 full workdays (9 hours/day)
- Due: **Wednesday 3:00 PM EST**

**If submitted late Friday:**
- Task goes Active: **Friday 5:00 PM EST**
- 1 hour counted (Friday 5-6 PM)
- Weekend doesn't count
- Due: **Tuesday 4:00 PM EST** (1 hour Mon + 9 hours Tue = 10 total, 38 remaining on Wed)

### 2.2 Excluded Holidays

The following holidays **do not count** toward the 48-hour timer:

#### U.S. Federal Holidays
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (3rd Monday in January)
- Presidents' Day (3rd Monday in February)
- Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
- Juneteenth (June 19)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (1st Monday in September)
- Thanksgiving Day (4th Thursday in November)
- Day after Thanksgiving (Friday after Thanksgiving)
- Christmas Eve (December 24, half-day)
- Christmas Day (December 25)
- New Year's Eve (December 31, half-day)

#### Indian National Holidays
- Republic Day (January 26)
- Holi (Varies, typically March)
- Good Friday (Varies, March/April)
- Ram Navami (Varies, March/April)
- Mahavir Jayanti (Varies, March/April)
- Labour Day (May 1)
- Buddha Purnima (Varies, May)
- Eid al-Fitr (Varies, April/May)
- Eid al-Adha (Varies, June/July)
- Independence Day (August 15)
- Gandhi Jayanti (October 2)
- Dussehra (Varies, September/October)
- Diwali (Varies, October/November)
- Christmas Day (December 25)
- Guru Nanak Jayanti (Varies, November)

**Holiday observance:** If a holiday falls on a weekend, we observe the substitute day (usually Monday for U.S. holidays). Indian holidays are observed on the actual date regardless of weekday.

### 2.3 Holiday Operations

**During holidays, our operations continue with potential delays.**

- **U.S. holidays:** Operations run normally from our U.S. office, but you may experience slight delays due to reduced team availability
- **Indian holidays:** Our design team in India observes these holidays, which may cause delays in task processing
- **Major holidays:** During Diwali, Holi, Christmas, or New Year periods, expect 24-48 hour delays

**Complete unavailability announcements:** If we're truly unable to operate (rare), we'll announce via:
- Social media (@trysako)
- Product banner notification
- Email to all active customers
- Status page: [status.trysako.com](https://status.trysako.com)

**SLA impact:** Holiday delays do not trigger automatic credits unless the total delay exceeds our 48-hour SLA.

### 2.3 When the SLA Clock Starts

The 48-hour timer starts when a task enters **"Active"** status:

| Scenario | When Clock Starts |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Composer-initiated task** | When you approve the AI draft direction and provide final assets |
| **Direct submission** | When you submit a complete brief with all required assets |
| **Task moved from backlog** | When we move it to "Active" (you'll get a notification) |
| **Revision requested** | When you submit feedback (new 48-hour clock) |

**What counts as "complete brief":**
- Clear description of what you need
- All required brand assets (logos, fonts, colors)
- Reference examples or inspiration (if applicable)
- Copy/text content (if applicable)
- Dimensions or technical specs (if applicable)

**Incomplete briefs:** If your brief is missing critical info, we'll move the task to **"Waiting on You"** and request clarification. Clock pauses until you respond.

### 2.4 When the SLA Clock Pauses

The timer **pauses** in these situations:

| Status | Why Clock Pauses | Resumes When |
|--------|------------------|--------------|
| **Waiting on You** | We need clarification, assets, or approval from you | You provide the requested info |
| **Revision Requested** | You've asked for changes, we're awaiting detailed feedback | You submit revision notes |
| **Paused Subscription** | You've paused your account | You resume your subscription |
| **Scope Change** | You've changed requirements mid-task (e.g., "actually, make it a video instead") | You approve new scope/timeline |
| **Third-Party Delays** | Waiting on your client's approval, external stakeholder, etc. | Approval received |

**Important:** The clock **does not pause** for our internal processes (reviews, revisions on our end, pod handoffs, etc.). Those are our problem, not yours.

### 2.5 When the SLA Clock Ends

The SLA is **met** when:
- **First draft is uploaded** to your dashboard
- **You're notified** via Slack/email
- **Task moves to "Review"** status

**First draft means:**
- Fully designed, production-quality asset
- Matches your brief and brand guidelines
- Ready for your feedback or approval
- Source files included (Figma, After Effects, etc.)

**Not considered "first draft":**
- Rough sketches or wireframes (unless that's what you requested)
- Incomplete designs missing key elements
- Designs that don't match the brief

### 3.1 Standard Tasks (48-Hour SLA Applies)

| Task Type | Examples | SLA Applies? |
|-----------|----------|--------------|
| **Landing pages** | Hero section, full page, mobile responsive | Yes |
| **Presentation decks** | Sales deck, pitch deck, investor deck (up to 10 slides) | Yes |

| **Email designs** | Newsletter templates, promotional emails | Yes |
| **Display ads** | Banner ads, social ads (standard sizes) | Yes |
| **UI mockups** | App screens, dashboard designs (1-3 screens) | Yes |
| **Motion graphics** | Social videos (15-60 sec), animated logos, GIFs | Yes |
| **Illustrations** | Icons, spot illustrations, simple graphics | Yes |

### 3.2 Complex Tasks (Custom Timeline)

For complex or large-scope tasks, we'll provide a **custom timeline** at task creation. This replaces the 48-hour SLA.

**Examples of complex tasks:**
- Multi-page websites (5+ unique pages)
- Long-form video (2+ minutes with animation)
- Extensive illustration sets (10+ custom illustrations)
- Brand identity packages (logo + full style guide)
- Presentation decks (15+ slides)
- Interactive prototypes with complex logic

**How it works:**
1. You submit the task
2. We review scope and provide estimated timeline (e.g., "5 business days")
3. You approve the timeline
4. Custom SLA applies instead of 48 hours

**Custom SLA credits work the same way:** If we miss the custom deadline, you get day credits.

### 3.3 Revisions

**Revision turnaround:**
- **Minor revisions** (color tweaks, copy changes): **24 business hours**
- **Major revisions** (layout changes, new sections): **48 business hours**

**Unlimited revisions** are included in your plan, but each revision starts a new SLA clock.

## 4. What Happens If We Miss the SLA

We take SLA misses seriously. Here's what happens:

### 4.1 Immediate Actions

**When we miss a deadline:**
1. **Automatic notification** sent to you and our team
2. **Task is escalated** to senior pod members
3. **Priority flag** added (your task jumps the queue)
4. **Direct communication** from your pod lead explaining the delay

### 4.2 Credit System

**Automatic credits applied:**

| Miss # (per billing cycle) | Credit Amount | Example |
|----------------------------|---------------|---------|
| **1st miss** | 1 day subscription credit | $50 credit (on $1,500/month plan) |
| **2nd miss** | 1 day subscription credit | $50 credit |
| **3rd miss** | 1 day subscription credit | $50 credit |
| **4th+ miss** | 1 day subscription credit each | $50 credit each |
| **Maximum per month** | 7 days total | $350 credit (on $1,500/month plan) |

**Credit calculation:**
- **Monthly plan:** (Monthly fee ÷ 30 days) × days missed
- **Annual plan:** (Annual fee ÷ 365 days) × days missed

**How credits are applied:**
- **Automatically tracked** by our system (no manual request needed)
- **Applied to next invoice** as a line-item discount
- **Email confirmation** sent when credit is issued
- **Visible in your billing dashboard**

**Credits don't expire** (as long as you remain an active subscriber).

### 4.3 Escalation Path

If you're experiencing **repeated SLA misses** (3+ in a month):

**Step 1: Automatic escalation**
- Your account is flagged for review
- Pod lead investigates root cause
- Action plan created to prevent future misses

**Step 2: Leadership involvement**
- Founders notified
- Direct communication with you to resolve issues
- Potential pod reassignment if needed

**Step 3: Service recovery** (if issues persist)
- Additional credits beyond standard SLA credits
- Free month or significant discount (case-by-case)
- Option to pause subscription without penalty

**Enterprise customers:**
- **Dedicated escalation contact** (direct email/Slack to pod lead)
- **Custom SLA targets** (if specified in your contract)
- **Monthly SLA reports** with performance metrics

## 5. SLA Exclusions

The 48-hour SLA **does not apply** in these situations:

### 5.1 Customer-Caused Delays

| Scenario | Why Excluded | Solution |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| **Incomplete brief** | Missing assets, unclear requirements | Provide complete info; clock restarts |
| **Late feedback** | You take 5+ days to review | No penalty; your timeline extends |
| **Scope changes** | Major changes after task starts | New timeline agreed upon |
| **Multiple revisions** | 5+ rounds of revisions | Each revision gets fresh SLA |
| **Unresponsive** | We can't reach you for clarification | Task moved to "On Hold" |

### 5.2 Force Majeure

SLA does not apply during events beyond our control:
- Natural disasters (earthquakes, hurricanes, floods)
- Pandemics or public health emergencies
- War, terrorism, civil unrest
- Government orders or restrictions
- Major internet/infrastructure outages (AWS down, etc.)
- Power outages affecting our team

**If force majeure occurs:**
- We'll notify you ASAP
- Provide estimated resolution timeline
- No SLA credits issued (but we'll work to minimize delays)

### 5.3 Account Status

SLA does not apply to:
- **Free trial accounts** (no paid subscription)
- **Paused subscriptions** (clock pauses, tasks on hold)
- **Accounts in arrears** (payment failed, outstanding balance)
- **Terminated accounts** (violations, cancellations)

### 5.4 Special Requests

SLA may not apply to:
- **Rush requests** (if we agree to expedited delivery, that becomes the new SLA)
- **Custom projects** outside standard scope (e.g., strategy consulting, we'll discuss timeline)
- **Experimental features** in beta (we'll set expectations upfront)

## 7. Quality Standards

Speed means nothing if quality suffers. Our SLA includes quality commitments:

### 7.1 What "First Draft" Means

**Every first draft includes:**
- **Matches your brief** (correct specs, content, style)

- **Brand-consistent** (uses your colors, fonts, logos correctly)

- **Production-ready** (no placeholder text, no missing elements)

- **Editable source files** (Figma, After Effects, PSD, etc.)

- **Export-ready** (PNG, JPG, MP4, or requested format)

**If first draft doesn't meet these standards:**
- Not considered "delivered" for SLA purposes
- We'll fix immediately and deliver a proper first draft
- SLA clock continues running

### 7.2 Revision Standards

**Revisions delivered within:**
- **Minor changes:** 24 business hours (color, copy, spacing)
- **Major changes:** 48 business hours (layout, new sections, rework)

**Unlimited revisions included** (we don't count or penalize you for revisions).
