This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of Sako ("Service"), operated by [Creative Ops, Inc.](https://www.creativeops.in/) ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

**Violations may result in immediate suspension or termination without refund.**

## 1. General Principles

You agree to use Sako only for lawful purposes and in ways that don't infringe the rights of others or restrict their use of the Service.

**Use Sako to:**
- Submit legitimate design and motion requests for your business
- Collaborate with your team on creative projects
- Build and maintain your brand assets

**Don't use Sako to:**
- Harm, harass, or defraud others
- Violate laws or regulations
- Infringe intellectual property rights
- Compromise security or privacy

## 2. Prohibited Content

You may not submit content that:

### 2.1 Illegal Content
- Violates any applicable law, regulation, or court order
- Promotes illegal activities or substances
- Facilitates fraud, money laundering, or financial crimes

### 2.2 Harmful Content
- Contains malware, viruses, or malicious code
- Designed to damage, disable, or impair systems
- Enables phishing, spam, or social engineering attacks

### 2.3 Infringing Content
- Infringes copyrights, trademarks, patents, or trade secrets
- Uses third-party assets without proper licensing
- Misappropriates others' likenesses or identities
- Violates publicity or privacy rights

### 2.4 Objectionable Content
- Promotes violence, terrorism, or hate speech
- Contains sexually explicit material involving minors
- Harasses, threatens, or defames individuals or groups
- Spreads false or misleading information designed to deceive

### 2.5 Regulated Content
- Promotes unregistered securities or investment schemes
- Advertises controlled substances without proper licensing
- Violates healthcare, financial, or other industry regulations

## 3. Prohibited Activities

You may not:

### 3.1 Security Violations
- Attempt unauthorized access to our systems or other users' accounts
- Probe, scan, or test vulnerabilities without authorization
- Interfere with service to any user, host, or network
- Forge headers or otherwise manipulate identifiers

### 3.2 Platform Abuse
- Circumvent usage limits, rate limiting, or access controls
- Use automated tools to scrape, harvest, or mine data
- Resell, sublicense, or redistribute the Service without authorization
- Use the Service for load testing or benchmarking without permission

### 3.3 Competitive Activities
- Use the Service to build a competing product
- Reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble Sako Composer or Sako OS
- Extract algorithms, models, or proprietary methodologies

### 3.4 Account Violations
- Share login credentials with unauthorized users
- Create multiple accounts to evade restrictions
- Impersonate others or misrepresent your affiliation
- Use accounts belonging to others without authorization

## 4. Intellectual Property

### 4.1 Your Obligations
You represent and warrant that:
- You own or have rights to all content you submit
- Your use of the Service doesn't violate third-party IP rights
- You have proper licenses for any third-party assets included in your briefs

### 4.2 Reporting Infringement
If you believe content on Sako infringes your copyright, contact us at legal@trysako.com with:
- Identification of the copyrighted work
- Description of the infringing material
- Your contact information
- Statement of good faith belief
- Statement under penalty of perjury that you're authorized to act

### 4.3 Our Response
We respond to valid DMCA notices by:
- Removing or disabling access to allegedly infringing content
- Notifying the accused party
- Terminating accounts of repeat infringers

## 5. Resource Usage

### 5.1 Fair Use
All plans include limits on active slots, team seats, and other resources. You agree to:
- Stay within your plan's resource limits
- Not exploit the Service in ways that degrade performance for others
- Use the Service only for its intended purposes

### 5.2 Excessive Use
We may limit, suspend, or throttle accounts that:
- Consume disproportionate resources
- Submit requests designed to game the queue system
- Otherwise abuse the Service's capacity

## 6. Export Controls

You agree not to use Sako in violation of:
- U.S. Export Administration Regulations
- International Traffic in Arms Regulations
- Economic sanctions administered by OFAC
- Any other applicable export control laws

This includes not:
- Exporting deliverables to embargoed countries
- Providing access to sanctioned individuals or entities
- Using the Service for prohibited end-uses (weapons, nuclear, etc.)

## 7. Enforcement

### 7.1 Monitoring
We may monitor use of the Service to:
- Ensure compliance with this AUP
- Protect the Service and our users
- Comply with legal obligations

We don't actively monitor content you submit, but we may review content when we receive complaints or detect violations.

### 7.2 Actions We May Take
If we believe you've violated this AUP, we may:
- Warn you and request corrective action
- Remove or disable access to violating content
- Limit, suspend, or terminate your account
- Report violations to law enforcement
- Pursue legal remedies

### 7.3 No Refunds
Accounts terminated for AUP violations are not eligible for refunds.

## 8. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of AUP violations, please report them to:

**Email:** legal@trysako.com  
**Subject:** AUP Violation Report
